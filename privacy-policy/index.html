Privacy Policy — Wallet Nomad

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Wallet Nomad is an offline expense tracker. This policy is short because the app does very little with your data — because it doesn't collect any.

What we collect: Nothing. Wallet Nomad has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. Your trips, expenses, categories, and settings are stored only on your own device.

Network activity: The app makes anonymous requests to two public exchange-rate services (open.er-api.com and a jsDelivr-hosted currency API) to fetch current and historical exchange rates. These requests don't include your name, device ID, location, or any other identifying information — they're the same kind of anonymous request your browser makes to any public website.

Data sharing: We don't share anything, because we don't collect anything to share.

Data deletion: Since nothing leaves your device, uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Contact: Questions about this policy can be sent to support@walletnomad.app.
